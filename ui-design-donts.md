# UI Design — Don't Do List
*Common "AI-generated" design tells to avoid*

## Icons & Emojis
- Overusing icons and emojis throughout the interface
- Icons/emojis that don't semantically match what they represent
- Icon boxes with uniformly rounded corners
- Teaser/feature boxes with an icon stuck top-left as a default pattern

## Typography
- The one serif font every AI startup defaults to
- Serif headline paired with sans-serif body copy as a reflexive combo
- Overly small font sizes and thin/light strokes
- Numbers set in serif oldstyle figures
- Faint, low-contrast monospaced font for labels/descriptors
- Four or five competing fonts with no clear system
- All-caps "eyebrow" text above headlines, especially in pill-shaped rounded borders
- Giant, bold, centered H1 dominating the hero section

## Color & Gradients
- Neon gradients on elements
- Neon gradient borders
- The default purple-to-blue gradient
- Purple/black/blue palettes as a reflexive "tech" choice
- Generic glassmorphism cards

## Shape, Borders & Depth
- Rounded corners applied to everything by default
- Oversized drop-shadow radii
- Boxes nested inside boxes inside boxes (colored + outlined)
- Text boxes with a colored border on one side (usually left)
- Vertical colored accent borders on one side of a box
- Pill-shaped borders used as a catch-all shape

## Layout, Spacing & Hierarchy
- Weak or absent visual hierarchy
- No sense of balance
- No real spacing system (inconsistent gaps) — or the opposite failure: perfectly even spacing everywhere with no intentional emphasis
- Inconsistent layouts from section to section
- Every inch of space filled in — no breathing room, feels cramped
- Trying to cram too many elements/ideas into one screen
- Decorative or contextual elements with no functional reason to exist
- Rows of matching stat counters (e.g. three side-by-side) as a default hero pattern
- Mixing genuinely well-executed elements with amateur ones in the same design (great icon/font pairing next to bad spacing, or great color use next to tiny illegible text)

## Navigation & Interactive Elements
- Dead or non-functional CTAs and buttons
- Illogical duplicate controls (e.g. two close/"X" icons in the nav)
- Shiny, glossy button styles
- Pop-up modals that block or overlap other components

## Motion & Effects
- Parallax backgrounds used with no functional purpose

## Framework Tells
- Visuals that read as generic, unstyled Tailwind CSS defaults
- Tailwind used without any custom design decisions layered on top

## Copy
- Vague, specifics-free marketing copy ("Empower your workflow")
- Letting AI write sales copy wholesale
- Excessive em-dashes

## Technical Polish
- No contact method with a real domain email
- Poor SEO fundamentals
- Leftover console logs in production
- Layouts that don't scale/respond across device sizes
